# SQL Sales Analytics

A **MySQL 8.0+ Sales Analytics project** focused on extracting business insights from customer, product, and sales data.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project covers:

* Database & dimension exploration
* Business KPI and magnitude analysis
* Product & customer ranking
* Time-series and cumulative analysis
* Year-over-Year performance analysis
* Customer & product segmentation
* Part-to-whole analysis
* Customer and product reporting views

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **MySQL 8.0+**
* SQL
* CTEs
* Window Functions
* Views

## 📊 Key Metrics

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Products
* Average Selling Price
* Revenue by Category
* Revenue by Customer
* Product Performance
* Customer Segmentation
* Year-over-Year Growth
* Customer Recency & Lifetime Value

## 🗂️ Database Structure

```text
dim_customers
      │
      │ customer_key
      ▼
 fact_sales
      │
      │ product_key
      ▼
dim_products
```

`JOIN` • `GROUP BY` • `CTE` • `CASE` • `WINDOW FUNCTIONS` • `RANK` • `LAG` • `SUM OVER()` • `AVG OVER()` • `TIMESTAMPDIFF()` • `CREATE VIEW`
